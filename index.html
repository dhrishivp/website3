Our kitchen

Menu highlights

A taste of what comes out of our kitchen. Spice levels can be adjusted, so just ask.

Tom Yum Soup

Our namesake: hot and sour broth with lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime and chilli, with king prawns.

Pad Thai

Stir-fried rice noodles with egg, beansprouts and spring onion, finished with crushed peanuts and lemon. With chicken or king prawns.

Tempura Prawns

King prawns in a light, crisp batter, served with sweet chilli sauce for dipping.

Vegetable Soup

A light, clear Thai broth loaded with fresh vegetables. A good vegetarian choice.

About us

A family kitchen on Sycamore Road

Tom Yum is a small, family-run restaurant with a friendly atmosphere. Our cooks make traditional Thai food from fresh ingredients, balancing hot, sour, salty and sweet the way it is done at home.

The dining room is cosy and fills up in the evenings, especially at weekends, so we recommend booking ahead.
